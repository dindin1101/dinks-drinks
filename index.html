How booking works

1

Pick date & slot

Choose Court Rental or Open Play, then tap your date and time on the live availability grid.

2

Add paddles

Renting paddles? Just toggle “Rent paddle? Yes” and choose how many you need.

3

Pay your way

Send the exact amount via GCash and enter the reference number — or choose Over the Counter and pay at the venue.

4

Show up & play

Save your booking reference code and present it when you arrive. Dink responsibly! 🥤

Please note: GCash bookings are confirmed once payment is verified. Over-the-counter reservations are held until 15 minutes past your start time.
